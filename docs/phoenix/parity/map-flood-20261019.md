# Native map request flood: before and after (2026-10-19)

Plan: `superpowers/plans/2026-10-18-phoenix-rails-visual-map-parity-plan.md`, group A.

## Setup

- Local compose stack `rcmap` on port 13096 with `DAWARICH_RAILS=off`. Its database is a fresh `dawarich_development` in the stack's own PostGIS container.
- The seed user holds 4 320 synthetic points around Leipzig (2026-10-10 … 10-16), from which 18 tracks were generated.
- Two Chromium contexts are signed in and have `/map?start_at=2026-10-10&end_at=2026-10-16` open:
  - a visible tab;
  - a tab whose `document.visibilityState` reports `hidden`.

  Requests are recorded after the map has loaded.
- Bus messages are counted with `redis-cli psubscribe '*'`. Database transactions are the `pg_stat_database.xact_commit` delta.
- Script: `.scratch/orch/rcmap/flood-check.sh` (recalculation) and `MODE=points` (live points, 1 per second).

## What changed

- **No progress broadcast.** Transportation progress no longer broadcasts on the Rails `tracks` stream. Rails reclassifies with `update_column` and broadcasts nothing.
- **Narrower subscriptions.** The map no longer subscribes to the imports, notifications and sharing topics. A native topic the chrome already holds is delivered once, and messages for topics the map doesn't hold skip admission.
- **Throttled admission.** The `/map` admission is reused for 5 s. Family, sharing, reconnect and the other map descriptors still re-admit on every message.
- **Rails refresh policy on the client:**
  - tracks → tracks tiles after 1 s;
  - points → point tiles, visited countries and the recent point after 1 s;
  - edits → immediately;
  - family → family reload after 1 s, with no 30 s poll;
  - imports and progress → nothing.

  A hidden tab defers everything until it is visible.
- **Lifecycle events.** `map:ready` and `map:failed` no longer reload the navbar.
- **Kept as before:** socket reconnects and map edits still do the immediate full refresh. A delayed reconnect joined the next edit batch and closed the point editor mid-save (found by G44, bisected on the mini).

## Remaining divergences from Rails, by design

- **Recent point.** It costs one `GET /native/map/data/points` per refresh, because receipts carry no coordinates (M6 contract). Rails reads the point from the broadcast payload.
- **Hidden tab.** It makes no requests at all. Rails' MapLibre loop pauses in hidden tabs, but its debounced timers still fire.
