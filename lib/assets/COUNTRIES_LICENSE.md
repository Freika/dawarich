# Country boundaries

`countries.geojson.gz` and the generated `public/maps/countries-v3.pmtiles`
contain Natural Earth 1:10m administrative country boundaries. Natural Earth
data is in the public domain. See https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/.

The source has been reduced to canonical country names and ISO 3166-1 alpha-2
and alpha-3 identifiers, with local corrections recorded in Dawarich history.
Crimea is filed under Ukraine (UA), matching the ISO 3166-2 subdivisions
UA-43 and UA-40, rather than under Russia as in Natural Earth's default edition.
